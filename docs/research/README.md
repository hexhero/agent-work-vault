# research — research notes

Technical research and comparisons: a judgement at a point in time that will go stale. Unlike [guides](../guides/README.md): this is "which one and why", guides is "how to use it".

## Conventions

- Sub-folders per topic.
- frontmatter: `type: research`, `aliases`, `topic` (its sub-folder), `updated` (last update date); the "Research" view of [overview.base](../../overview.base) shows them all with their dates.
- Numbers in research (stars, versions, prices) go stale fast; state the research date in the text and say where to check current values.
